The Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program

The Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program offers shareholders of Freelancer Limited (ASX:FLN) (OTCQX:FRLCY) certain benefits including reduced fees on a range of Freelancer services.

Benefits at a Glance

Fee Rebates on Freelancer.com

A rebate of 5% of project and contest commissions will be credited to your Freelancer.com account, calculated monthly, for each full calendar month that you remain eligible in the program.

Priority Customer Support

You get priority customer support from our 24x7x365 multilingual support team.

Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Badge

A unique badge will appear on your Freelancer.com and WarriorForum profiles, signifying you are a shareholder of the company.

Access Your Benefits

These benefits are available to individuals with a minimum of 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares. Simply click the button below and fill out the application form to start accessing your benefits.

Where to Purchase Shares

The ticker symbol for Freelancer is ASX:FLN for anyone purchasing shares from Australia.

Note:
Under ASX rules called 'Minimum Marketable Parcel' (MMP), your first purchase of FLN (or any ASX-listed company) must be at least A$500 worth of shares (excluding brokerage fees), regardless of which broker you use.

Terms and Conditions

To be eligible for the Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program, you must meet the following:

  • A shareholder must be a validly registered user of either the Freelancer.com or the Warrior Forum website (Freelancer User). Suspended accounts will not be eligible. Freelancer Users who breach the Terms of Use of either website (as set out in the User Agreement) may have their Program membership suspended or cancelled, at the discretion of Freelancer Limited.
  • A shareholder must be a natural person(s) and:
    • owns 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares; or
    • is nominated by a company, trust or incorporated body that owns 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares. A company, trust or incorporated body can nominate one natural person to receive the full benefits of the package using the Nominee Appointment Advice form (pdf), a scanned copy of which must be attached to the online application form. A separate appointment form must be completed where more than one entity is registered in a joint holding.
    • A shareholder must hold 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares for an entire calendar month to be eligible for shareholder benefits in that calendar month. Fee rebates will not be paid for any month and fee discounts will not be available where a shareholder's holding falls below 5,000 ordinary fully paid Freelancer Limited shares.
    • Rebates are paid on project and contest commissions (not upgrades), and only for fees that are paid. Unpaid fees will not receive rebates.
    • Freelancer Limited may vary the benefits, terms and conditions of the Freelancer Foundation Shareholder Program at any time, including by updating this page.
